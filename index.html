Air Ticketing

       

DOMESTIC & INTERNATIONAL AIR TICKET BOOKING

       

ATT has been entrusted with the responsibility of air ticketing for official travel of Government of India             Institutions and LTC vide order number No. 31011/5/2014-Estt (A.IV) issued by DoPT on, 21/08/2017. At             present, ATT is issuing tickets for official travel of Government departments as well as the personal             tickets for LTC travel.             ATT has been servicing various Ministries, Government departments, PSUs, Defence forces, Paramilitary forces             etc for many years. Recently, ATT has launched a customised self-booking platform which works as a one stop             solution for the client. It has facilitated the Government departments to get the tickets issued on their             own with minimal human intervention. At present, this portal and the credentials have been shared with             multiple Government organizations.             ATT has the in-house expertise to provide air ticketing solutions for almost all the airlines- Domestic and             International.
